# 2.6 Impact scale

Basis: Financial impact levels are set as a share of The Alajo's capital reserves of ₦3 billion, the buffer available to absorb losses. Severe starts at 20% of capital, a loss that would seriously threaten the company's ability to keep operating. The low appetite for loss of customer funds, exposure of customer data and regulatory breach is applied through the appetite threshold: any score of 10 or more is above appetite, so an impact 5 risk is above appetite even at likelihood 2. Customer bands are based on 650,000 registered customers.

Impact 1, Negligible
Financial loss: under ₦3 million (under 0.1% of capital)
Customers affected: under 100
Service disruption: under 1 hour
Effect: negligible financial loss, no regulatory or customer impact

Impact 2, Minor
Financial loss: ₦3 million to ₦30 million (0.1% to 1% of capital)
Customers affected: 100 to 1,000
Service disruption: 1 to 4 hours
Effect: local service degradation, fixed internally

Impact 3, Moderate
Financial loss: ₦30 million to ₦150 million (1% to 5% of capital)
Customers affected: 1,000 to 10,000
Service disruption: 4 to 24 hours
Effect: significant disruption to a business unit, reportable incident

Impact 4, Major
Financial loss: ₦150 million to ₦600 million (5% to 20% of capital)
Customers affected: 10,000 to 65,000 (up to 10% of customers)
Service disruption: 24 to 72 hours
Effect: critical outage, regulatory inquiry and customer churn

Impact 5, Severe
Financial loss: above ₦600 million (over 20% of capital)
Customers affected: over 65,000
Service disruption: over 72 hours
Effect: systemic failure, major data breach, sanctions or licence risk

Rule: a risk takes the highest impact level reached on any one dimension.

Likelihood, bands and appetite are unchanged and follow the RiskTrack "How scoring works" page: score is likelihood times impact (1 to 25); Critical 15 to 25, High 10 to 14, Medium 5 to 9, Low 1 to 4; scores of 10 or more are above appetite.
